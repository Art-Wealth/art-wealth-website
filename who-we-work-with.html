Who we help

Real Melbourne lives. Real financial decisions.

We don't pretend to be everything to everyone. The clients we work with best tend to fall into one of four groups — and they all share one thing: they want clear, plain-English advice from someone who actually knows them.

🚀
Persona 01

Ambitious young professionals

Late 20s to mid 30s, earning well, and starting to feel like you should be doing more strategic things with your money — but not sure where to start.

What's on your mind

  • "Should I be salary sacrificing into super?"
  • "How much insurance do I actually need at my age?"
  • "Is investing in ETFs the right move, or should I focus on the deposit?"
  • "How do I make sure these earning years aren't wasted?"

How we typically help

  • Set up appropriate cover while premiums are cheap
  • Build a salary-sacrifice and contribution strategy that works with your tax
  • Start a separate, diversified investment plan outside super
  • Map out the path to a property deposit or financial independence
👨‍👩‍👧
Persona 02

Growing families & dual-income households

Mid 30s to late 40s, juggling a mortgage, kids, school fees, two careers and not enough hours. You know you should have a plan — it just keeps getting pushed down the list.

What's on your mind

  • "If something happened to one of us, would the family be okay?"
  • "Are we paying way too much tax — and is super being optimised?"
  • "How do we balance the mortgage, kids' education and retirement?"
  • "We've got bits and pieces all over the place — can we tie it together?"

How we typically help

  • Calculate proper personal insurance needs for both income earners
  • Consolidate and optimise super, often saving thousands in fees
  • Build a household cashflow and goal plan
  • Tax-effective contribution strategies (including spouse contributions and splitting)
🌅
Persona 03

Pre-retirees & retirees

55 to 70, eyeing the next chapter. You've worked hard, accumulated a decent base, and want clarity on whether you can comfortably retire — and how to make your savings last.

What's on your mind

  • "Do we actually have enough — or do I need to keep working?"
  • "How do I turn my super into reliable income?"
  • "Will we get any Age Pension, and when?"
  • "What about tax, aged care, leaving something for the kids?"

How we typically help

  • Model multiple retirement scenarios so you know exactly where you stand
  • Set up Transition to Retirement (TTR) strategies from age 60
  • Structure tax-effective pension-phase income
  • Coordinate Centrelink, aged care and estate planning
💼
Persona 04

Small business owners & SMSF investors

40s to 60s, running a business or managing your own super. You're an expert in your own world — but your personal financial planning has taken a back seat for years.

What's on your mind

  • "All my wealth is tied up in the business — how do I diversify?"
  • "Am I making the most of my company structure for super and insurance?"
  • "Should I have an SMSF? Should I buy a property through it?"
  • "What's my exit plan, and when can I actually step back?"

How we typically help

  • Key person and personal insurance through the business, tax-effectively
  • Maximise super through your company structure
  • SMSF setup and LRBA-style property strategies where appropriate
  • Long-horizon business succession and exit planning
A few signs we'd be a good fit

You'll probably feel at home with us if…

You want plain-English advice — not jargon and product brochures.
You'd rather work directly with one experienced adviser than a call centre or rotating juniors.
You value being explained the why behind a recommendation, not just the what.
You like the idea of a long-term partnership, not a one-off transaction.
You want fees that are clear, agreed in writing, and not driven by product commissions.
You appreciate a tax-aware approach — and an adviser who'll work with your accountant.
Don't fit a box?

Most people don't fit perfectly into one category.

A young professional starting a family. A pre-retiree who's also a business owner. A migrant family building wealth from scratch. The personas above are useful starting points, but real life is messier — and that's exactly the kind of work we do.

If you're not sure whether we're a fit, the easiest way to find out is to have a quick chat. The discovery meeting is free and there's no obligation.
